## Full description (both stores; well under Android's 4000-char limit)

GC ETA estimates when your employment-based green card priority date is likely
to become current — built from ten years of published Visa Bulletin data, not
guesswork.

WHAT IT DOES
Enter your country of birth, priority date, and I-140 category. GC ETA gives
you a likely date range, how confident that range is, and how many people
with earlier priority dates are ahead of you in the same category and
country — measured from certified labor certification records, not assumed.

HOW IT'S BUILT
- A simulation of how the cutoff has historically moved for your exact
  category and country, adjusted for the actual visa supply available each
  year.
- A direct count of people ahead of you, from the government's own labor
  certification data, cross-checked against the trend-based estimate.
- A plain accounting of what's scheduled to happen next — annual resets,
  court orders, processing pauses — and what would move your estimate in
  either direction.

WHAT IT'S HONEST ABOUT
The app states its own accuracy plainly, including where its estimates have
historically been wrong, and does not turn its queue count into a promised
wait time when the data doesn't support that division. It shows a range and
its confidence, not false precision.

PRIVACY
Nothing you enter leaves your phone. There's no account, no analytics, and no
tracking of any kind. The app downloads one public data file and works from
there.

Not affiliated with the U.S. government. Not legal advice.
