Step 1 of 3
Where were you born?
Birth country sets your column, not citizenship. Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan count separately from mainland China. A spouse's birth country can be used instead; a child's cannot.
Step 2 of 3
What is your priority date?
Printed on your I-140 approval notice. For PERM cases it is the day the Labor Department received the PERM, not the day it was certified. For EB-1 and NIW cases it is the day USCIS received the I-140.
