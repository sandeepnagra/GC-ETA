Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

GC ETA does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

What you enter into the app

Your country of birth, priority date, immigrant visa category, and the other details you enter to get an estimate stay on your device. They are never sent to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party. The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no user identifier of any kind.

The one network request the app makes

On launch, the app checks for an updated copy of the public government data it is built on (Visa Bulletin cutoffs and related figures) by requesting a fixed, parameter-free file from our own published data feed. This request carries no identifiers, no query parameters, and nothing about your device or your case. Everyone who uses the app requests the exact same file.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics software development kit (SDK), no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting or usage-tracking service of any kind. Nothing about how you use the app is measured, recorded, or shared, by us or by anyone else.

Data from your device

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device data or permission beyond what is required to run as an ordinary app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
