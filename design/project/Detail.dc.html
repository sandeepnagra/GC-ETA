The queue is everyone in EB-2 India with a priority date earlier than yours: pending adjustment applications at USCIS, applicants waiting at the National Visa Center, and people with an approved I-140 who have not filed yet. Spouses and children count, because each uses a visa number.
Each year India can use its 7% share of EB-2, plus whatever other countries leave unused, plus whatever falls down from EB-1. Dividing the queue by that supply gives the wait, and the spread of past years gives the range.
Inventory data is published one to three months late, and how many approved I-140 holders never file is not knowable. Both widen the range.
Sources: USCIS pending I-485 inventory (5 Aug 2026), NVC annual waiting list (1 Nov 2025), USCIS I-140 approvals by country (FY2026 Q3).
