The basic idea
Each month the State Department publishes one cutoff date for your category and country. The day that cutoff passes your priority date, you are current and a visa number can be used on your case.
What we look at
1
How fast the cutoff has moved. Ten years of monthly bulletins, including the jumps each October and the stalls each summer.
2
How many people are ahead of you. Applications already filed with earlier dates, measured against the visa numbers your country can expect each year.
3
What could interrupt it. Processing pauses, country bans and bills in Congress, each with an effect we have written down in advance.
Why a range, not a single date
The cutoff moves in monthly steps and leaps only a few times a year, usually in October. A single day would look precise and be wrong. We show the window we have confidence in and the most likely point inside it, and we widen the window when the data is thin.
